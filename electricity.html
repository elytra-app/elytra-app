Key ideas

  • Current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor, measured in amperes (A).
  • Voltage is the "push" that drives current, measured in volts (V).
  • Resistance opposes current flow, measured in ohms (Ω).
  • In a series circuit, current is the same everywhere but voltage splits across components.
  • In a parallel circuit, voltage is the same across each branch but current splits.
